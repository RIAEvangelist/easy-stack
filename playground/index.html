What to notice
  • Pending work is displayed top-first.
  • Cooperative tasks drain synchronously through this.next().
  • Manual tasks stay active until another Next hand-off.
  • Releasing stop does not resume work by itself.
  • Clear preserves flags; Reset restores constructor defaults.
  • The editor follows visual actions until you begin a custom edit.
